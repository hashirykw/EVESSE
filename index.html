Stop blaming the water. Check these five

Hair loss is often written in your bloodwork first. These are the levels Dr. Faria looks at before recommending PRP, QR678® or anything else.

Ferritin

1730
Low · < 17 ng/mLThinning hair and increased shedding
Better · > 30 ng/mLStronger hair growth

Reference ranges differ between labs. Your results are read with you at consultation — this is a guide, not a diagnosis.
