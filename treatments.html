Skin & facials

Glow that holds up in daylight.

Most skin plans start here: clean the surface, calm what’s inflamed, then treat tone and texture in that order. Prevention is easier than correction — especially with pigment, which gets harder to lift once it settles in.

  • Signature Rejuvenating FacialInstant brightness, deep hydration and a refined texture — the treatment patients book before an event.
  • Chemical peelsLifts existing pigment and evens tone. Strength is chosen for your skin type and season.
  • Pigmentation & melasma careVitamin C, daily SPF and in-clinic peels together — prescribed, not guessed.
  • Acne & acne scarsTreat active breakouts medically first, then resurface the marks they leave.
  • Glass-skin plansA course of facials, boosters and home care for a lasting, even glow.

Injectables

Look rested — not changed.

Every injectable at Evessé is placed by a doctor after a face assessment. The aim is balance: softer lines, defined lips, a lighter jaw — and a face that still moves like yours.

  • Botulinum toxinSoftens frown lines, forehead lines and crow’s feet.
  • Dermal fillersRestores volume and contour — cheeks, chin, jawline.
  • Lip definitionHydration, a sharper vermilion border, natural shape.
  • Barbie toxToxin in the trapezius muscle for a longer, slimmer neck line.
  • Masseter slimmingA softer jaw for those who clench or grind.

Lifting & collagen

Firmness, without surgery.

Fine lines are often the first sign that collagen production is slowing. The good news: early prevention makes a lasting difference. These treatments work in the dermis to rebuild support.

  • Non-surgical thread liftTargets sagging skin, lifts and boosts collagen in one session.
  • MicroneedlingControlled micro-injury that stimulates new collagen.
  • Skin boostersHydration delivered deep into the skin for bounce and glow.
  • Retinol & home carePromotes cell turnover between clinic visits.

Hair restoration

It starts with your bloodwork.

Shedding is often a symptom, not the disease. Before any procedure, Dr. Faria reads five markers — ferritin, vitamin D, B12, zinc and TSH — and treats what she finds. Then the in-clinic work begins.

  • Hair-loss assessment & bloodworkScalp examination plus the five blood markers.
  • PRPYour own platelets, concentrated and placed into the scalp to support growth.
  • QR678® & DutexomeGrowth-factor and exosome-based protocols for thinning hair.
  • Scalp microneedlingImproves delivery and stimulates the follicle.
  • Laser hair removalFor unwanted hair on face and body.

Body & weight

Lighter, fitter, measured.

Weight care at Evessé begins with a body composition assessment rather than a scale: BMI, body fat percentage, visceral fat, muscle mass and a metabolic health score. The plan follows the numbers.

  • Body composition analysisKnow where you stand before you start.
  • Medical weight managementDoctor-led plans with follow-up.
  • Body contouringShape and tone for stubborn areas.
  • Free evaluation daysAnnounced on Instagram for both clinics.

Bridal skin

Six months to the glow.

Bridal skin is a timeline, not a facial. Starting early leaves room to clear acne and pigmentation first, so the final weeks can be all about radiance.

  1. 6 months outConsultation, skin analysis, treat acne and pigmentation.
  2. 3 months outPeels, boosters and collagen work; hair and scalp if needed.
  3. 1 month outInjectables if planned — time for them to settle.
  4. 2 weeks outSignature Rejuvenating Facial and hydration.
  5. Wedding weekGentle glow treatment only. Nothing new.

Before you book

Do I need a consultation first?

Yes. Every plan at Evessé starts with a consultation so treatment matches what your skin, scalp or body actually needs.

How do I book?

Message the clinic nearest to you on WhatsApp — DHA on 0300-1172177 or North Nazimabad on 0333-5974283 — and the front desk confirms your slot.

Can I see prices online?

Treatment fees depend on the plan, so they’re shared after consultation. The front desk can tell you the consultation fee.

Who performs the treatments?

Plans are set by Dr. Faria Khan, Consultant Dermatologist. Injectables and procedures are carried out by doctors.

Is there downtime?

Many treatments have none. Where there is — some peels, microneedling — you’ll be told before you book.
